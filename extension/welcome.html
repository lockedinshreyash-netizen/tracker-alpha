Alpha

Every new tab is now
your day.

Your streak, today’s hours and one button to start. Everything else in your browser works exactly like before.

What changed

Your new tab page. It shows your Alpha day instead of a blank page. That’s the only thing.

What didn’t

Search. The address bar and the search box use your usual search engine. Same results.
Bookmarks, history and passwords. Untouched.
Every other website. We can’t see them, and we don’t look.
Your settings and other extensions. Untouched.

Good to know

iChrome said this can “replace the page you see when opening a new tab”. That’s the whole point. It also said it can “read your data on trackeralpha.in”. That’s only so your day can be copied from Alpha to this tab, on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
iIf Chrome asks “Change back to Google?” on your next tab, click Keep it. It asks that for every new-tab extension.
iChanged your mind? Remove extension is in the menu (•••) on every new tab. Your old new tab comes straight back, and your Alpha data stays in Alpha.
