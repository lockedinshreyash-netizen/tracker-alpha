Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Cancellation

Paid items on Alpha, such as the Access Pass, are one-time payments. There is no subscription and nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refunds

If you are not happy, email lockinhq@gmail.com within 7 days of payment with your Google account email and payment ID. We will refund the full amount.

If you were charged twice or charged by mistake, we refund the extra payment at any time.

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. When a refund is made, the paid access is removed.

Shipping

Everything we sell is digital and available in your account right away. Nothing is shipped.
